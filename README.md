# SanitasLink

SanitasLink is a self hosted platform for managing a medical practice: patients, appointments, prescriptions and staff — all in one place, with privacy and security built in from the start.

It is designed for **general practitioners and their teams**. A practice (an "office") gets its own private workspace, and everyone in the team — the lead physician, collaborating doctors and secretaries — only ever sees the data of their own practice.

## Features

- **Practice workspace** — every practice gets its own secure space, with staff managed through invitations rather than shared passwords.
- **Team roles** — lead physician, collaborating physician, and secretaries with different levels of access. The practice owner decides who can do what, and roles can be adjusted at any time.
- **Patients** — register patients, keep their contact details and clinical notes, and manage privacy/consent records.
- **Appointments** — book, reschedule and manage the daily agenda, with recurring appointments, availability management and automatic email/SMS reminders.
- **Prescriptions** — secretaries can prepare prescription requests; physicians review and sign them; then they can be printed or sent to the patient, with deeper integration with national e-prescription services.
- **Patient portal** — a self-service area where patients can book appointments, view their prescriptions and update their own details.
- **Smart agenda & telemedicine** — availability management, reminders, video visits and remote consultations.
- **Reporting & analytics** — practice activity, prescriptions and patient follow-up insights.
- **Extended clinical record** — medical history, allergies, diagnoses and exam results in a structured clinical record.
- **Platform administration** — a central console to manage practices, roles and the permission catalog.
- **Security & privacy by design** — each practice's data is isolated at the database level, sensitive health data is encrypted, every important action is recorded in an audit trail.

## Getting started

The platform is still in active development. The backend — identity, security, tenancy, audit and the clinical features described above — is implemented and covered by an automated test suite.
